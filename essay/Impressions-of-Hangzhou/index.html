Impressions of Hangzhou

I have many opportunities to come to Hangzhou But I have been reluctant to come Because I’m afraid of being obsessed with her So that you can’t live in other cities

It’s like if your first RPG game is Legend of Sword and Fairy Your first online game is World of Warcraft Then you will be tasteless when you play other games…

If not for this business trip I don’t think I will come here in my life I’m afraid it will ruin her impression in my heart I’m even more afraid that I can’t live without her

Fortunately She didn’t disappoint me at all Unfortunately I fell in love with her all of a sudden

Hangzhou I agree with it everywhere Culture, food, humanities And that fascinating landscape

Let alone the well-known West Lake Walk a random street in the old town Even if it is an industrial zone The bulletin boards on the roadside all contain elements of Chinese style, and there will be all kinds of rocks on the corner of the grass. Even outside the door of an industrial design institute, they grow bamboo As long as you pay attention, there are scenery everywhere

There is a small bar downstairs in the hotel where I live This is not a tourist area Very different from Shanghai There is a mixture of Chinese and English, you look as if she is Chinese, not an exotic product

We often say The biggest feature of the Chinese is the national melting pot He will tolerate and assimilate foreign cultures And what I saw in other cities can only be said to be Chinese-style Westernization In Hangzhou, it’s a thorough integration.

Hangzhou traditional dishes It came from Kaifeng at a glance Xiaolongbao, cat ears, small wontons And Henan and Shandong restaurants everywhere

Hangzhou is in the same line as Shanxi and Henan Mainly pasta Hasn’t changed in a thousand years

Hangzhou women have the sophistication of Shanghai women With the boldness of a woman from the Central Plains Woman passing by on the street Wear two more tops and bottoms. A short top is called neon. Jeans are rarely seen on the lower body. Most of them are cloth skirts and gauze skirts, which are called clothes

There are a lot of people showing their belly But don’t show your belly button You don’t seem to think they dress up like this to attract the eyes of the opposite sex But I think it should be dressed like this here

Rarely, Hangzhou women are amazing at first sight More of it is that you look at her walking past as if you have blended into the scenery As elegant, chic and beautiful as Hangzhou

白蘋紅蓼西風裡 一色湖光萬頃秋

It takes early morning to go to West Lake, there are few people Set foot on Beishan Road at 6 o’clock Watching the sun gradually rise Feel the lake breeze on your face Listen to the birds chirping Smell the fragrance of sweet-scented osmanthus If the lake is foggy, you will want to blend in here

Compared with the pavilions in West Lake I prefer the quietness of Lingyin The winding road extends up the mountain Both sides of the mountain road are full of tea trees Every turn is an ingenuity The more you go up the mountain, the stronger the fragrance of osmanthus The more you go to the mountains, the less distracting thoughts in your heart will be. I have turned the mountain road ten times, and I also have the urge to stay here ten times

Rent a yard, it doesn’t need to be big Make a pot of tea without thickening Entertain guests and friends, not much Keep a dog, don’t have to be expensive Stay with your parents, watch your children, and live your life like this with your wife.
